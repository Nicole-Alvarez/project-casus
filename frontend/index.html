 MOVEMENT GUIDE

Find a way upward.

Explore freely. Glowing lanterns set the resting place where R returns you. No collection or finish goals.

A / D · ← / →
Walk and steer in the air.
Space / W / ↑
Jump, then release and press again for an air jump. The gallery steps need both.
Hold Shift
Float beneath your balloon cape while falling. Drift from the gallery to the small ledge across the hollow.
Hold toward a wall
Cling while descending. Jump launches away and restores one double jump and air dash. Hold toward the wall and press Q to dash upward. Release and press Q again after cooldown to keep climbing. Release the wall to fall.
Hold E
Pull toward the highlighted silk anchor. Release and press again to chain anchors. Jump cancels the tether.
Press Q
Dash horizontally, or upward when holding into a wall. Invulnerable for the active 0.09-second dash; one open-air dash before landing or wall jumping and a 0.8-second cooldown. Wall dashes can repeat while holding against a wall; release and press Q again after cooldown. Dash restores one air jump, usable during the dash. Jump or collision ends it.
R
Return to your last lantern.

Gallery → silk ascent → quiet sanctuary. Jump and dash across the broken bridge, or climb the shaft. On the right return ledge, step left to descend to the lower corridor.

Your sword is cosmetic. This map is for exploring and testing movement. Opening menus pauses your controls while friends continue. ARTWORK & SOUND

Below the roots.

The four insect explorers, whole-character movement sheets and cavern background were generated for this project from the approved character concepts. Smooth terrain, arches, roots and lanterns are drawn in Canvas.

Sound effects: Kenney New Platformer Pack, CC0.

Generated source images, prompts, frame metadata and hashes are included in the assets folder. Hollow Knight and Silksong are visual inspirations; this project is unaffiliated.

There are no enemies or damage hazards. Dash invulnerability remains part of the shared multiplayer simulation.
