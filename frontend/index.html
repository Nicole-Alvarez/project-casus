MADE WITH FREE ASSETS

A shared little world.

Mosslight is a cooperative browser platformer for up to eight explorers per room. Your server keeps everyone in the same world.

Art & sound

Kenney Pixel Platformer supplies the pixel art. Kenney New Platformer Pack supplies sound effects. Both are CC0.

Downloaded originals, categorized files, original licenses, and a source manifest are included in the project's assets folder.

Controls

A / D or ← / → to walk. Space / W / ↑ to jump. R to respawn. Touch controls appear on touch devices.
